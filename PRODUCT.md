# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (chosen by Thibo). Static site, deployed from the `main` branch of `thibovonderlynckllc/tv-portfolio`.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring teams evaluating Thibo for a web developer role. They arrive from a CV or LinkedIn link, skim for a minute or two, and want proof he can design and ship real products.

Secondary: business owners looking for someone to build their website, who need enough trust to send an email.

## Product Purpose

Thibo Vonderlynck's personal portfolio. It should get him a developer job first, and still let freelance work come in. Success is a recruiter remembering him afterwards and contacting him or downloading the CV.

## Positioning

A developer who also designs, and who co-founded and ships a real startup: Spiromni, which builds 360° virtual tours of workplaces. Spiromni leads the site; the rest of the work follows as proof.

## Operating Context

Visitors open the site from a CV, LinkedIn or a job application, on desktop at work or on a phone. They look at the lead project, scan the other work, check experience and education, then either download the CV or email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page plus one detail page per project (10 projects).
- Sections that carry over: all 10 projects, testimonials, experience and education, skills overview, contact, CV download.
- Contact is an email address (thibovonderlynckllc@gmail.com) with copy-to-clipboard; there is no contact form.
- Links: GitHub (github.com/ThiboVonderlynck), LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/thibo-vonderlynck-9654a52bb).
- Site language is English.
- The redesign must not be committed, pushed or deployed until Thibo says so; the live site is being viewed by recruiters.

## Brand Commitments

- Name shown as "Thibo" / "Thibo Vonderlynck".
- Clean break from the previous look (dark background, orange gradient headings, centred section titles, card grids).
- Must not feel safe and forgettable, and must not feel playful or gimmicky either: a corporate recruiter should still take it seriously.
- Thibo compared the film-strip look with a plain "clean professional" version on 2026-10-03 and preferred the film-strip styling.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project screenshots: poster and full-page captures per project in `src/assets/projects/`.
- Portrait: `src/assets/pfp.webp`.
- Three client testimonials with avatars (Aron, FocusTrading; Maxim, Mavo Productions; Joe, Omega Trading) in `src/assets/testimonials/`.
- CV: `public/cv.pdf`.
- Experience: internships at Ask Maeve (Ghent, 16/02/26 to 06/06/26) and Storything (Kortrijk, 11/03/24 to 14/06/24). Education: Howest MCT (2024 to 2026) and Howest WDD (2022 to 2024).
- Spiromni: co-founded with Maxim and Pieter-Jan; first client project is a virtual tour for Oleon in Ertvelde. No 360° panorama imagery is in this repo; do not fabricate tour footage, clients or figures.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the founder story: Spiromni first, everything else as supporting proof.
2. Show shipped work, with real screenshots and live links, over claims about skill.
3. Memorable but credible: take a visual risk a hiring manager would still respect.
4. Fast and readable; a recruiter gets the picture in under a minute.
