Audience and job: recruiters first, freelance clients second. They should remember Thibo as the developer-designer who co-founded Spiromni, then download the CV or email.

## Direction contract

On 2026-10-03 Thibo tried a plain professional version and a Dennis Snellenberg style rows version, and asked for this film version back both times.

THESIS: The work is film and prints on a light table, and Spiromni is the frame that got picked.

OWN-WORLD: Cool backlit glass ground (#e6ecee), one black 35mm film strip in the hero with sprocket holes that show the table through, amber edge print (#f0c53a) in condensed caps, red grease pencil (#e3271e) for marks. Work shown as white-mounted prints in varied sizes. Archivo variable: expanded heavy for display, condensed for edge print. Hairline rules, 2 to 3px corners.

STORY: Sees the name and the boxed Spiromni frame; scans the prints; reads who he is and where he worked; the table switches off and the email is the one thing lit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky label bar with four links and CV. Name in one full-width line of expanded caps on desktop (two lines on phones). Intro at left and CV button at right. Below, a full-bleed film strip with Spiromni as the first frame, boxed in red pencil.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Filtering turns unpicked prints into negatives. The strip advances on load, then the pencil box draws. No hover-scroll, loupe or draggable card: Thibo removed those.

OPEN: Thibo said this version's sections do not look like one styling (film strip in the hero, prints in Work). A quieter pass that stripped the prints, portrait strip and labels was tried on 2026-10-03 and reverted at his request. Unresolved.
